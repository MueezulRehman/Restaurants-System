Inventory Management

Track and manage your pharmacy inventory

What is Inventory?

Inventory refers to all the items, goods, and materials a company holds for the purpose of reselling them for a profit. These are considered a crucial business asset and are tracked for financial reporting.

  • Management: Effective inventory management balances having enough stock to meet customer demand with the costs of holding excess inventory.
  • Valuation: The value of inventory is a key part of a company's balance sheet.

Supplier Management

Manage your pharmacy suppliers and vendor relationships

Who are Suppliers?

Suppliers are the companies or vendors that provide a business with the goods, raw materials, and components necessary for its inventory. The relationship between a business and its suppliers is a key element of the supply chain.

  • Relationship Management: Building strong, reliable relationships with suppliers is crucial for a smooth supply chain.

Purchase Management

Manage inventory purchases and supplier orders

What are Purchases?

Purchases refer to the process of ordering and buying inventory from suppliers. This process is central to inventory management and involves deciding what to buy, how much to buy, and when to place an order.

  • Purchase Orders (POs): A purchase order is a document sent to a supplier detailing the items, quantities, and agreed-upon prices.

Returns Management

Process customer returns and supplier returns

What are Returns?

Returns involve the movement of inventory back to the seller. This can happen for various reasons, such as damaged goods, overstocking, or defects.

  • Supplier Returns: When a business returns inventory items to the supplier.
  • Customer Returns: When a customer returns a purchased item.

What are Reports?

Reports are organized summaries of a company's inventory data at a given time. They are critical for monitoring stock levels, analyzing performance, and making informed business decisions.

  • Inventory on Hand: Shows the current stock count and its value.
  • Inventory Performance: Identifies best-selling and worst-selling products.
  • Low Stock/Reorder: Lists items that are falling below a preset reorder point.

What is a Store Profile?

A Store Profile is a detailed collection of information about a specific retail location. It is used to analyze a store's unique characteristics to optimize its operations, marketing, and inventory.

  • Data Points: Includes location, size, customer demographics, and performance data.
  • Customization: Retailers use store profiles to tailor product assortments.
